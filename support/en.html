whyme · Support

whyme keeps your favorite apps in one place and opens them with a tap. Free — no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads. No sign-in. Available in Simplified Chinese and English.

Features

  • App library & search — a built-in catalog of 2,000+ apps, searchable by name, pinyin or initials; search the App Store for anything missing and add it.
  • Categories & shortcut bar — rename, add, remove and reorder categories; up to 7 shortcut slots for apps or categories.
  • Tap to open — opens apps via iOS URL schemes / universal links. If an app can't be opened, whyme stays put and shows "Can't open this app right now — reported."; it never jumps to the App Store automatically.
  • Share to add — in the App Store, Share an app → whyme.
  • Scan — use whyme's own QR scanner, or pick an installed app's scanner under Settings → Scan provider.
  • Built for iPhone Duo — layouts for the closed (outer) screen, unfolded portrait/landscape, and regular iPhones; left/right-hand mode and 3 or 4 columns on the outer screen.
  • Wallpapers — built-ins grouped into Fold & Open and Scenery; Fold & Open wallpapers show a different picture when closed and when open. Or use your own photo.
  • Dynamic Island Return — after opening another app, jump back to whyme from the Dynamic Island (can be turned off).
  • Backup and Restore — export/import a JSON backup.
  • Optional "DUO打开器" Shortcut — off by default; only usable after you import a Shortcut with that name; used only when a direct link fails.

FAQ

  • An app won't open? Many apps have no public launch link. whyme reports the app’s App Store ID, region and name automatically (no personal data); once we add a way to open it, a library update fixes it — no app update needed.
  • Does whyme read my installed apps? No. It never reads your installed-app list or changes your Home Screen. "Installed" detection only covers a small declared set of apps and may miss some.
  • Where is my data? On your iPhone. Only feedback you submit, automatic reports for apps that can't be opened or are missing (App Store ID, region, name), the push token for library updates, and App Store searches sent to Apple leave the device. See the Privacy Policy.
  • New phone? Settings → Backup and Restore → export, then import on the new phone.
  • Language? Settings → Language: Follow System / 简体中文 / English.
  • Why camera access? Only to scan QR codes; nothing is uploaded.
  • Is it free? Yes — completely free, no IAP, subscriptions or ads.
