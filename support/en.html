Quick help

  • An app won’t open: Without a launch URL, Zhenghe falls back to the App Store page. Availability depends on your App Store account region.
  • Share to add: Use the system Share sheet → Zhenghe to add an App Store link to your library or Installed list.
  • Language: Settings → Language → Follow System / Simplified Chinese / English.
