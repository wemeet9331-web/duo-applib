What we collect

Zhenghe is primarily an on-device organizer and launcher. By default:

  • On-device only: categories and layout, shortcut rail, wallpapers and history, apps added via Share, the Installed list, language preference, and similar settings. This stays on your device (system backups such as iCloud Backup are handled by Apple) and is not uploaded to our servers through normal use.
  • Network requests (no personal profile upload):

- Apple iTunes Search / Lookup for public metadata on App Store links; - Our signed app-library manifest (and signature) on GitHub Pages.

  • Camera: only when you use Scan, to read QR codes; images are not uploaded to our servers.

We have no accounts, no ad tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs, and we do not sell user data.

Optional features (if enabled later)

An optional “help improve open methods” switch would upload minimal app fields (e.g. App Store ID, storefront, name) only after you opt in. Off by default.

Retention and deletion

Clear data in-app or by uninstalling (excluding system backups). The remote catalog is public library data, not your personal layout.

Children

Not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect children’s personal data.
