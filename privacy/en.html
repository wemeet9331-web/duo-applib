whyme Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-18 Contact: weimikehu@gmail.com

whyme is a free app organizer and quick launcher. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or ads, and you don't need an account or sign-in. This policy covers only what whyme actually does.

1. Stored only on your device

The following stays in whyme's app container (and in the App Group shared with its Share extension and widget). None of it is uploaded to us.

  • Your app entries, categories (including custom names), shortcut bar, recents and search input.
  • Your settings, such as language, columns, hand mode and the Dynamic Island Return switch.
  • Wallpapers: the built-in one you chose, or photos you picked (compressed and stored on device), plus a history of up to 20 photos you've used.
  • Apps you added from the Share sheet.
  • Any JSON backup you export. You decide where it goes.

Deleting whyme removes all of this. Copies inside iOS device backups follow Apple's backup rules.

2. Permissions

  • Camera is requested only when you use Scan. QR codes are decoded on device and nothing is uploaded. If the result is a URL, it opens only when you tap "Open link".
  • Photos uses the system photo picker. whyme receives only the images you select and never asks for access to your whole library.
  • Notifications: whyme shows no notifications and never asks for notification permission. It uses invisible silent pushes only to refresh the app library (see 3.3).
  • Dynamic Island / Live Activity: after you open another app, an optional "Return" Live Activity shows that app's name on your device. You can turn it off in Settings.

3. Information sent over the network

3.1 Feedback and feature requests you submit

When you tap Submit, our server in Singapore (report.whyme.vip) receives:

  • your text;
  • an optional contact (leave it blank to stay anonymous);
  • up to 3 screenshots, compressed on device with EXIF/GPS metadata removed;
  • the whyme version, device model code (e.g. iPhone19,4, not your device name), iOS version, region and language settings, a random ID used only to de-duplicate this submission, and the time.

We use this only to handle your feedback. Items that fail to send are queued on your device and retried. Please don't include sensitive information.

3.2 Automatic reports for apps that won't open or are missing from the library

whyme automatically sends a short report to the same server in two cases: when you tap an app it can't open, and when an app you share to whyme isn't in its library.

Each report contains only the app's App Store ID, the App Store region (two letters, e.g. us, cn), the app's name, the whyme version, and the event type ("can't open" or "missing"). The same app and event are reported only once.

Reports never include your name, account, contact details, device name or model, OS version, advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV), location or contacts, and they never include a list of your installed apps. We use them only to improve the app library. This is always on and can't be turned off.

3.3 Library updates and silent push

whyme downloads the signed app-library manifest from GitHub Pages (wemeet9331-web.github.io) when it launches and when it returns to the foreground, and verifies the signature before using it. No personal data is sent with this request.

To refresh promptly, whyme sends its Apple push token, push environment, app version and locale to our server. They are used only for silent update pushes.

3.4 Apple's public services

When you search for apps or use App Store links, your search term or the App Store ID, plus the selected region, go directly to Apple's iTunes Search/Lookup API (itunes.apple.com). App icons load from Apple's image servers (e.g. is1-ssl.mzstatic.com). Apple's privacy policy applies to these requests, and we never receive your searches.

3.5 Opening other apps

whyme asks iOS to open apps through URL schemes or universal links. The app or website that opens has its own privacy practices.

4. Server, logs and retention

  • Server: report.whyme.vip is hosted in Singapore, and everything is sent over HTTPS.
  • IP addresses:
  • Our database does not store IP addresses.
  • Access logs record only the time, request path, status and size, with no IP.
  • Rate limiting uses a one-way hash of your IP that is kept in memory only, never written to disk, and expires within 24 hours.
  • Server error logs may record a connecting IP and are used only for troubleshooting.
  • Retention:
  • Archived access and error logs from the report.whyme.vip reverse proxy are kept for up to 14 days.
  • The server's system service log records only the time, request path and status code, with no IP address or personal information. It is cleared automatically on the system's default schedule.
  • Feedback is kept for up to 12 months from when we receive it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.
  • Automatic reports are stored as per-app aggregates with no personal data and are kept for up to 24 months.
  • Push tokens are deleted as soon as they become invalid, or after 12 months with no update.

5. What we don't do

We use no ad or analytics SDKs and do no cross-app tracking. We never sell or rent data or use it for ad targeting.

6. Your choices

  • If you don't submit feedback, nothing in section 3.1 is ever sent.
  • You can turn off Dynamic Island Return in Settings.
  • You can delete your local data, or the whole app, at any time.
  • To access or delete feedback you sent, email weimikehu@gmail.com with when you sent it or a short summary. We reply within 30 days.

7. Children

whyme is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Significant changes will also be noted in the release notes.
